# UrbanPlanToolbox v1.8.4 settings and About modernization

- Modernizes the Settings cards outside update management while preserving the app's existing WinUI 3 card backgrounds, borders, and theme resources instead of changing the overall visual material.
- Reorders Settings by usage logic as Appearance & language → Background & startup → Project notifications → Data management → App maintenance, with adaptive vertical layouts on narrower windows.
- Reworks the About page's app information, project/open-source, and privacy/legal cards, keeps the v1.8.3 update-management card style, and removes the Diagnostics & logs card.
- Unifies Data Management and WebDAV actions on one standard button appearance and one standard button-text foreground, removing mixed accent-button treatments.
