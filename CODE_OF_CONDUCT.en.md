# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | [日本語](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ja.md)

## Our commitment

We are committed to maintaining a welcoming and inclusive community that respects different backgrounds and experience.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Focus on issues and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Accept good-faith feedback and remain professional when opinions differ.
- Respect privacy, data boundaries, and the project's local-first principle.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, malicious disclosure of personal information, deliberate disruption of discussion or contributions, and other conduct unsuitable for the community are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates this code and may limit or end participation. Report conduct requiring attention to the maintainers through GitHub's private channel. For security vulnerabilities, follow the [Security Policy](SECURITY.en.md).
